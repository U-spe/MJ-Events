How It All Began

At MJ Events & Designs, we believe that life's most precious milestones deserve more than just ordinary setups—they deserve unforgettable luxury landscapes. What started as helping friends and family craft intimate birthday themes quickly evolved into a dedicated studio setting standards across grand spaces.

We approach every venue with a fresh perspective, combining sophisticated floral arrangements, tailored structural themes, and seamless timeline planning to ensure your dream aesthetic is achieved flawlessly.

Meet The Designer

Marcia Jack

Founder & Creative Director

Hello and welcome! I'm Marcia Jack, the creative mind behind MJ Events & Designs. My obsession with spatial styling and design aesthetics started long before it became my profession. To me, a blank venue room isn't empty space—it is an open canvas waiting to tell a spectacular, human story.

Over the years, I’ve turned my passion for design and decorating into a business focused on creating events that feel personal and memorable. Whether you’re planning a small gathering or a large celebration, I work closely with you to bring your vision to life in a way that reflects your style and personality. From the first idea to the final details, I’m committed to making the process smooth, enjoyable, and stress free while creating a space that you and your guests will never forget.
