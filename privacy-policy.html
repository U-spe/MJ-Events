1. Information We Collect

MJ Events & Designs collects multiple layers of data to maintain operational integrity, complete detailed structural calculations, execute design blueprints, and process transactional requests securely. This data is split into explicit categories:

A. Information You Voluntarily Disclose

When interacting with our structural capture systems (including the Consultation Booking Page and the Order Cancellation Request Portal), we collect specific identifier metrics. This incorporates your full legal name, authenticated electronic mail addresses, direct cellular communication numbers, specific target event calendar coordinates, accurate guest counts, venue geographical variables, design vision briefs, and milestone investment parameters.

B. Device and System Logs

Our infrastructure automatically captures network technical variables when navigating our site. This processing records your unique Internet Protocol (IP) address, localized browser engine variations, specific operating system environments, referring uniform resource locators (URLs), user behavior paths, clickstream timestamps, and specific navigation interactions across our layout architecture.

2. Processing Framework & Legal Foundations

We process your metrics strictly under verified lawful contexts. Our primary processing frameworks encompass:

  • Contractual Execution: Processing credentials to formulate project quotes, confirm spatial bookings, adjust production scope, and finalize logistical parameters.
  • Legitimate Interests: Optimizing layout loading times, identifying security vulnerabilities, monitoring form errors, and preventing fraudulent system activities.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with state business registries, corporate tax records, and structural safety audits.

Your inputs will never be aggregated, packaged, leased, or distributed to independent advertising networks or unverified broker ecosystems.

3. Formspree Processing & Third-Party Integrations

To safely route dynamic form transmissions, our infrastructure links directly with specialized API data relays. Specifically, our data capture pipelines connect directly to Formspree Inc. endpoints.

When you submit an active request via our booking or cancellation frameworks, your data is securely packed, encrypted, and processed directly through Formspree's routing channels before landing in our secure internal studio operations manager. Formspree maintains rigorous independent security protocols and processes data strictly according to our direct instructions. They are contractually barred from utilizing your submission metrics for any alternative operational goals.

4. Cookies, Identifiers & Digital Tracking

Our platform uses microscopic system tokens known as cookies to improve user experience and analyze client drop-off points. These data layers help us remember form fields during multi-step processes and track which layout modules you interact with most.

You can modify your browser settings to completely reject all browser cookies or block specific tracking files. However, if you disable these storage systems, certain high-performance dynamic features—including form persistence and layout state retention—may not function as intended.

5. Structural Security Architecture

MJ Events & Designs treats user security as a foundational component of our code base. All data transmission channels on this platform use Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS) layer systems alongside advanced Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption protocols.

While we use enterprise-grade cloud parameters to prevent data leaks, unauthorized access, or accidental alterations, no system can guarantee absolute safety. We highly encourage clients to use secure network environments when transmitting sensitive event configurations or personal metrics.

6. Data Retention Scales

We store your data files only as long as necessary to complete your custom design projects, manage your active client account, fulfill regulatory tax duties, or resolve contract disputes.

General lead inquiries submitted through our Formspree endpoints are scrubbed from active systems after 180 calendar days if no formal agreement is signed. Formally finalized contracts and associated layout design records are kept securely for up to 7 years to meet corporate audit and liability compliance metrics.

7. Your Sovereign Data Rights

Depending on your geographic location, you hold strong legal protections regarding your personal data. These rights include:

Right of Access

You can request complete digital printouts of all personal information our studio currently holds regarding your files.

Right to Erasure

You can request the permanent destruction of your personal file records from our active cloud storage nodes, provided they don't conflict with pending contract completions.

Right to Rectification

You have the absolute authority to immediately correct inaccurate fields, outdated phone contacts, or corrupted event detail parameters.

To invoke any of these structural data privileges, please initiate a formal request through our primary studio communication infrastructure.

8. Operational Inquiries & Governance

MJ Events & Designs reserves the right to make structural updates to this privacy setup to match new technology deployments, legal guidelines, or workflow changes. We recommend checking back regularly to review any minor updates.

If you have any questions about how your data is handled, please reach out to our primary data compliance team through our verified online customer channel.
